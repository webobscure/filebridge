# FileBridge logo

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool, using `assets/remoter-fs-icon-v2.png` as
the visual reference. Saved unchanged as `assets/filebridge-icon.png` and used as
the extension icon. Earlier logo files are retained in the project and excluded
from the new VSIX.

## Final prompt

Use case: logo-brand. Edit target: the existing blue-and-white remote-files app icon. Rebrand it as FileBridge by replacing the central folder-with-arrows emblem with ONE clever, minimal integrated folder-and-bridge symbol. Retain the recognizable vivid blue rounded-square tile and white geometric design language. The white emblem should read simultaneously as a file folder and a bridge: a folder-tab silhouette along its top edge, a broad horizontal bridge deck, and one large simple arch of negative blue space below that deck, leaving two clear sturdy white piers. Make the bridge metaphor immediately legible without adding a separate bridge illustration. Remove the two old transfer arrows. Only one simple integrated mark, no stacked illustrations, no tiny details. Large bold shapes, balanced centered placement and comfortable margins, designed to remain legible at 32 and 48 pixels. Flat consistent blue and pure white, no gradients, no highlights, no textures. Fully opaque blue tile and white mark; only the area OUTSIDE the smooth rounded-square tile is transparent. Especially keep the entire upper blue field perfectly uniform and opaque, with no pale marks or translucent haze. No lettering, no initials, no watermark, no checkerboard pattern, no mockup. One finished square production PNG icon.

Transparent background: enabled.
